# Notion

Productivity and knowledge management platform. Started as a wiki/docs tool, now expanding aggressively into AI agents and workflow orchestration. Makes Notion (workspace), Notion AI, Custom Agents, and the new Developer Platform (Workers).

## Summary

Notion completed a $270M private equity tender offer at $11B valuation in January 2026. Not a new raise — existing shareholder liquidity. CEO: Ivan Zhao. ~800 employees, headquartered in San Francisco. Launched Developer Platform in May 2026, positioning as an AI orchestration hub.
