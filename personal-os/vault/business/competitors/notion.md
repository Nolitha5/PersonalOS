# Notion

Productivity and knowledge management platform. Started as a wiki/docs tool, now expanding aggressively into AI agents and workflow orchestration. Makes Notion (workspace), Notion AI, Custom Agents, and the new Developer Platform (Workers).

## Summary

Notion completed a $270M private equity tender offer at $11B valuation in January 2026. Not a new raise — existing shareholder liquidity. CEO: Ivan Zhao. ~800 employees, headquartered in San Francisco. Launched Developer Platform in May 2026, positioning as an AI orchestration hub.

## Latest Intel (2026-06-09)

- **Funding**: $270M PE tender offer at $11B valuation (Jan 2026). No new rounds. Acquired notion.com domain (June 3, cash-and-equity, 8-year process).
- **Product**: Notion MCP expanded — any member can build connections (was Workspace Owners only), 91% more token-efficient for DB creation. Custom Agent governance controls (per-agent credit limits, workspace caps). 100M+ users.
- **Hiring**: ~800 employees.
- **Leadership**: Ivan Zhao (CEO), Akshay Kothari (COO, announced notion.com acquisition). Stable.
- **Pricing**: Seat-based, 4 tiers — Free, Plus, Business, Enterprise.
