# LangChain

AI agent orchestration framework and platform. Makes LangChain (framework), LangGraph (agent orchestration), LangSmith (observability), and Agent Builder (no-code agents). Competes at the infrastructure layer for AI workflows and agent pipelines.

## Summary

LangChain raised $125M Series B at $1.25B valuation in April 2026 from ServiceNow, Workday, Cisco, Datadog, Databricks, and Frontline. Now a unicorn. CEO: Harrison Chase. 325 employees as of May 2026.
